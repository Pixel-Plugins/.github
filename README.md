## ✦ Orbit

### Build. Manage. Extend. Deploy.

**Orbit** is PixelPlugins' next-generation platform for building and operating modern digital experiences.

Instead of stitching together a CMS, frontend framework, hosting platform, integrations, and custom development workflows, Orbit brings them together into one extensible platform.

From a single environment, teams can create and manage websites, content, experiences, integrations, and custom functionality — while keeping infrastructure and deployment complexity behind the platform.

> **Orbit is currently developed as a private PixelPlugins platform.**

### What Orbit is designed for

- **AI-assisted development** — create and evolve digital experiences through intelligent development workflows.
- **Visual composition** — build sophisticated, responsive experiences from reusable sections and layouts.
- **Content management** — manage websites and content without requiring developers for every change.
- **Custom functionality** — extend experiences with purpose-built features and applications.
- **Multi-site architecture** — operate multiple websites and customer experiences from a shared platform.
- **Cloud deployment** — designed for modern cloud infrastructure and scalable production workloads.
- **Integrations** — connect external services, APIs, data sources, and business systems.
- **Governance & isolation** — architecture designed around controlled access and separation between customer environments.
